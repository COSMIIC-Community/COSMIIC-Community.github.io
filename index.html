What is the COSMIIC System?

Now available as open source, the COSMIIC System is a no-strings-attached neuromodulation platform available for adoption by researchers and startups. The system can be configured with existing stimulating and sensing modules or with new modules to support unique research requirements. Uniquely, the design, documentation, and regulatory precedence enable a speedy path through benchtop, chronic animal, and first-in-human studies.
