Privacy Policy

This policy describes what information the COSMIIC website (cosmiic.org) collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. Last updated: August 2026.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information that you do not voluntarily provide. When you contact us by email, we receive the information included in that message.

If you create an account or log in using your email address, we collect your email address and the date and time of account activity (such as sign-in events). This information is used solely to authenticate your identity and manage your account. We do not use it for marketing.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects information sent by your browser, including pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, approximate geographic location (derived from IP address), device type, and browser type. This data is aggregated and used only to improve the site.

Cookies

Google Analytics places cookies on your device to distinguish unique visitors and track sessions. These cookies do not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How We Use Information

Aggregated analytics data is used solely to understand site traffic and improve content and usability. Email addresses collected during account registration or login are used only for authentication and account management. We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC. Google may process analytics data on servers outside your country. Google's data practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

External links on this site (such as docs.cosmiic.org, community.cosmiic.org, and GitHub) are governed by the privacy policies of those respective services.

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted. Account email addresses are retained for as long as your account is active and deleted upon account closure. Email correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, contact us at open_source@cosmiic.org.
